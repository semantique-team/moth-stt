# Plan — Silero VAD as the no-speech guard (0.4.0)

> Written 2026-10-30. Decision taken by the author: **adopt a small pre-trained VAD**, replacing
> the energy guard, and accept the package at ~19.3 MB. This file is the handoff: a new session
> implements it. Live state: `STATUS.md`.

## Why

The ship gate requires **0 words on non-music noise/silence** (`docs/05` §5). The energy guard
(E-011 addendum) fixed digital silence (240 → 0 words) but cannot separate loud noise from speech:
E-016 emits **31 non-music words** with the LM (35 greedy) on the 240 non-music clips.

Two other routes were considered:

- **Noise augmentation with empty targets**: the principled fix, but our noise evaluation set *is*
  the only MUSAN audio we have, so training on it would be leakage. It needs a re-download of MUSAN
  (~11 GB), a file-level train/eval split, re-running every baseline on the new eval set, and a
  ~4 h run — and it still would not guarantee zero.
- **Energy / spectral-flatness VAD**: measured, does not separate (mel-spread overlap is large).

## Measurement (2026-10-30, done)

Silero VAD v5 ONNX (MIT, **2,327,524 bytes**, sha256
`1a153a22f4509e292a94e67d6f9b85e8deb25b4988682b7e174c65279d8788e3`). Per clip: fraction of 32 ms
windows with P(speech) > 0.5. Script: `runs/E-011/vad_feasibility.py`
(`uv run --with silero-vad python runs/E-011/vad_feasibility.py`).

| Set | n | min | p10 | median | p90 | max | gated at 0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T S1+S2 | 415 | **0.410** | 0.618 | 0.746 | 0.870 | 0.961 | **0.0%** |
| FLEURS-IT | 300 | **0.420** | 0.608 | 0.728 | 0.838 | 0.916 | **0.0%** |
| MUSAN noise | 120 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | **0.038** | **100%** |
| MUSAN music | 120 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.134 | 0.990 | 85.8% |
| silence | 120 | 0.009 | 0.009 | 0.009 | 0.009 | 0.009 | 100% |

Rule: **speech iff fraction > 0.10**. Margin is 4× below the weakest speech clip and 2.6× above
the noisiest noise clip. A *max-window* rule does **not** work (noise transients reach 0.99).

**Invocation trap** (cost one afternoon): the v5 ONNX takes **512 new samples + 64 of context**,
i.e. a **576-sample** input, with `state` `[2, 1, 128]` float32 and `sr` int64 scalar; it returns
`output [1, 1]` and `stateN`. Passing 512 samples silently produces ~0.001 on speech. The official
wrapper (`silero_vad.load_silero_vad(onnx=True)`, then `model(chunk, 16000)`) handles the context;
a hand-written port must keep the last 64 samples of the previous window.

**Level note**: our recordings are quiet (~−42 dBFS RMS) but the VAD works without normalisation
(max prob 1.000 on T); do not add a gain step.

## Decision

- Adopt Silero VAD as the **only** no-speech guard; the energy guard becomes redundant (the VAD
  gates 100% of silence) and should be removed or kept as a cheap pre-filter with a comment.
- Size: 17.11 + 2.33 = **19.44 MB** (hard ceiling 20). If it crosses 20 after packaging,
  int8-quantise the VAD and re-run the table before shipping.
- License: **MIT**, add the Silero notice to `stt/export.py::build_notice` and `NOTICE`.
- The VAD runs on **16 kHz PCM**, before the mel: in the browser the PCM already exists after
  `decodeTo16k` / the mic worklet.

## Implementation steps

1. **Vendor the model**: `stt/assets/silero_vad.onnx` (2.33 MB, sha256 above), committed with the
   MIT notice. Re-download with
   `curl -sL -o stt/assets/silero_vad.onnx https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad/raw/master/src/silero_vad/data/silero_vad.onnx`
   and verify the hash.
2. **`stt/vad.py`**: `speech_fraction(audio_16k) -> float` and `has_speech(audio, threshold=0.10)`
   using onnxruntime with the 64-sample context; lazy session so tests that do not need it stay
   fast. Unit tests: T clip (speech), noise clip (gated), silence (gated) — use a small committed
   fixture or synthetic signals, not the private recordings.
3. **Eval integration**: `runs/E-005/eval_sets.py` gains `--vad-guard` plus an audio root per set
   (T: `recordings/audio`, noise: `data/raw/noise`, FLEURS: `data/raw/fleurs/...`, CV:
   `data/raw/cv_it_27/...`); guarded clips get an empty hypothesis and are counted in `decode`.
   Sets without audio fall back to no gate with a warning.
4. **Browser**: `web/vad.js` (same context logic, `onnxruntime-web`), `release/config.json` gains
   `vad: {file, window: 512, context: 64, threshold: 0.10}`, `web/ort.js` runs it before the mel
   and returns `guarded: true` with an empty transcript; the file is added to `files` with its
   sha256. Playwright: the synthetic tone fixture must be gated; a speech fixture must not.
5. **Re-measure** (decode-only, no training):
   - noise set, both decoders, `--vad-guard`: **accept 0 non-music words** (silence already 0);
   - T-400 and FLEURS-IT: **0 clips gated** (the measurement says 0/415 and 0/300 at 0.10);
   - browser RTFx with the VAD in the loop; guard and mic tests.
6. **Repackage 0.4.0**: `stt/export.py` (unchanged acoustic graph) + the VAD file + config + NOTICE;
   report the total size and the browser table.
7. **Docs**: E-011 addendum (the VAD numbers and the before/after word counts), `docs/06` §1 scope
   (the guard is now a VAD, not just an energy check), `docs/11` decision D25 and threats, STATUS.

## Acceptance

- Noise set: **0 words on the 240 non-music clips** with greedy and with beam+LM; music reported.
- Speech: **0 gated** on T-400 and FLEURS-IT at threshold 0.10; no WER change on speech sets.
- Browser: 6/6 Playwright tests, ≥10× RTFx on 1 and 4 threads, package **≤ 20 MB**.
- Apnea: no change to the acoustic model, tokenizer, LM or α.

## Risks and fallbacks

- **onnxruntime-web refuses the graph** (opset/dynamic shapes): quantise to int8 or fall back to a
  WASM re-export; if that fails, keep the VAD Python-only and document the gap.
- **Size > 20 MB**: int8 quantisation of the VAD (~0.6–1 MB), re-run the table, accept if the
  separation holds.
- **Latency**: 312 VAD calls per 10 s; measure. If it costs >15% RTFx, run the VAD on 16 kHz mono
  with a stride of 2 windows? Do **not** skip windows silently: it changes the rule.
- **False rejects in the wild** (far-field, very quiet speech): threshold 0.10 is conservative on
  our data; document it and keep the rule in one place.

## Do not

- Do not train on `data/manifests/noise.jsonl` (it is the evaluation set).
- Do not tune the threshold on T (the table above already used T for *separability*, not for
  choosing the operating point; if the threshold changes, justify it on dev/noise only and say so).
- Do not change the LM, α or the acoustic checkpoint in this step.
- No commits or pushes without the author's explicit request (`AGENTS.md`).
